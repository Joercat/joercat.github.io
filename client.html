Reader mode

Fetches the page through your relay and extracts the text here — useful for sites that refuse to be proxied or are simply too heavy to render.

What works, and what can't

Works: articles, docs, wikis, forums and most server-rendered sites with their real CSS, images and fonts; in-page links, Back/Forward, GET and POST forms, and sessions — the relay keeps a cookie jar per host, so log-ins can survive.

Can't: Google, Cloudflare challenges and banks fingerprint datacenter IPs and will refuse; WebSockets and live video (a socket cannot be re-headed through an HTTP relay); and your browser's own cookies for the real site are never sent — the relay keeps its own jar instead.

Escape hatch: ctrl-click or middle-click any link to open the genuine page directly.
